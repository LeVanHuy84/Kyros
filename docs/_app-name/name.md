# Project Name

## **Kyros**

**Kyros** is the official name of the AI Executive Assistant platform. The name is inspired by the ideas of **clarity, intelligence, and purposeful action**, representing an AI that not only understands information but also helps users make better decisions and execute tasks efficiently.

Kyros is envisioned as a **Personal AI Operating System**—a trusted digital companion that connects knowledge, memory, planning, and automation into a seamless experience.

# App Icon Concept

# Canva Icon Design Prompt — Kyros

Design a **modern, minimalist app icon** for an AI Executive Assistant named **Kyros**.

The icon should **not** use common AI clichés such as robot heads, chat bubbles, brains, or circuit boards. Instead, it should communicate **intelligence, organization, trust, and orchestration** through simple geometric forms.

### Concept

Create a clean **hexagonal emblem** containing a **stylized letter "K"** formed by interconnected lines and negative space. The symbol should represent Kyros as the central intelligence that connects memory, planning, knowledge, calendars, tasks, and AI agents into one unified system.

### Style

- Modern SaaS / Apple / Notion inspired
- Flat vector design
- Minimal geometric shapes
- Symmetrical and balanced composition
- Premium, timeless, and professional
- Easily recognizable at small sizes (32×32 to 1024×1024)

### Color Palette

- Primary: Deep Indigo (#4F46E5)
- Secondary: Cyan (#22D3EE)
- Background: White or Dark Navy
- Avoid excessive gradients, shadows, or glowing effects

### Brand Personality

Elegant · Intelligent · Reliable · Calm · Professional · Future-ready

### Avoid

- Robot icons
- Chat bubbles
- Human faces
- Brain illustrations
- Complex circuit patterns
- Overly futuristic or cyberpunk aesthetics
- Cartoon or gaming styles

The final icon should feel like it belongs to products such as **Linear, Notion, Raycast, Arc Browser, or OpenAI**—simple, memorable, and instantly recognizable as a premium AI productivity platform.
